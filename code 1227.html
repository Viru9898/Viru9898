Roadmap to grow and earn for your future plans

A general plan you can adapt. Change the amounts and dates to match your own situation.

Foundation

Months 0–3

  • Write down your goals with amounts and dates, for example "₹5 lakh for a business in 3 years".
  • Track your income and expenses for one month.
  • Build an emergency fund of 3–6 months of expenses.
  • Pay off high-interest debt first (credit cards, personal loans).

Build earning power

Months 3–12

  • Pick one marketable skill: coding, digital marketing, video editing, design, writing, sales, accounting or a trade.
  • Learn from free or cheap sources such as YouTube, NPTEL, Coursera and freeCodeCamp.
  • Build 2–3 real projects or work samples.
  • At a job, ask for more responsibility. As a student, look for internships.

Add income streams

Year 1–2

  • Freelancing: Upwork, Fiverr, LinkedIn or local clients.
  • Side business: reselling, tutoring, content creation or a service.
  • Online income: YouTube, blogging or digital products. Slow to start, but they can scale.
  • Aim for 20–30% of your income from a second source.

Save and invest

Start early, even small

  • Save at least 20% of every income and automate it.
  • Start a monthly SIP in a diversified index fund, even with ₹500–1,000.
  • Take health insurance and term life insurance.
  • Avoid get-rich-quick schemes, "guaranteed return" offers and stranger tips.

Scale

Year 2 onwards

  • Reinvest profits in better skills, tools or a small team.
  • Raise your rates as your reputation grows.
  • Move from trading time for money toward building a business or assets.

Quick rules

  1. Consistency beats intensity.
  2. Spend less than you earn, always.
  3. Invest in skills first. They grow every other income source.
  4. Review your progress every 3 months.

This is general guidance, not financial advice.
